Rules

why it's built this way
  • Modify every jump you're not certain of. The bench box jumps and Bulgarian plyo come late in workouts when you're already fatigued. A missed landing at 42 costs months. Modifiers exist for every move.
  • Keep the deficit under 500. A 2025 network meta-analysis found lean mass preservation falls off past that. Yours is 450.
  • Protein does the protecting. An AJCN trial found roughly 2.4 g/kg beat 1.2 g/kg for fat loss while holding lean mass in a deficit. You're at 2.1.
  • Stack 5er Abs on strength days. Dedicated core work is thin in this program outside Core Circuit, which only appears in recovery weeks.
  • Short rest means lighter weight. That's the design, not your failure. Fifteen seconds between moves caps what you can load. Chase the reps at pace, not a personal record.
  • Measure your waist, not just your weight. BMI would call you overweight and it cannot tell muscle from fat. Waist-to-height ratio can. If the scale stalls but the waist keeps dropping, the program is working.
  • Reassess at week 3. If you're not down about a pound a week, cut 150 calories. No more than that.
